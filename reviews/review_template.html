Murder on the Orient Express

by Agatha Christie

Genre: Classic Fiction

Date Read: July 2026

Rating: ⭐3.5/5

Why We Chose This Classic

This month, the club decided to tackle Fitzgerald's masterpiece. While most of us read it back in school, revisiting it as an adult completely changed our perspectives...

Our Club Discussion

We spent nearly an hour debating Jay Gatsby's choices. Half of the room felt deeply sympathetic toward his romantic idealism, while the other half viewed him as a cautionary tale of obsession...

My Personal Take

The prose remains utterly unmatched. Every sentence feels meticulously polished. If you haven't picked this up since high school, I highly encourage you to give it another look.
