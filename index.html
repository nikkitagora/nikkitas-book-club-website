📚 Welcome to Nikkita's Book Club

Tracking my reading journey, one book at a time.

How It Started

I started a Book Club in March 2026 for family & friends to come together, read and share opinions on the books we read monthly.

How It's Grown

What started off as a casual conversation between a couple of cousins about wanting to read the same books, quickly became an idea to begin a Book Club where we read a book per month and share our thoughts and opinions with each other in a Whatsapp group or at an in-person meet up.

This small group chat quickly expanded, with new people wanting to join our Book Club every month.

We do weekly progress checks of the books we read and at the end of each month we share our ratings out of 5 with a written review. Books are suggested by Book Club members and voted on in a poll to decide which to read.

Why I Created This Site

Following the success of my Book Club, I wanted to create a website where I post my own reviews of each book we read, as well as any other books I read myself.

So here is my very first website, completely coded by me! I hope you all enjoy hearing my book reviews.
